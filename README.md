This case study is based on a request from real clients.  One of the clients is a petroleum engineer, and the other is an economist.  In short, they want to examine the decision-making of several companies associated with the onset of the COVID-19 pandemic.  Specifically, these companies operate oil and gas wells but were faced with difficult decisions due to unprecedented economic conditions related to the pandemic.  Of particular interest is behavior associated with decisions to "shut in" (or close / plug) gas wells. There are a number of reasons (e.g., if they expect the price of gas to be higher in the future) as well as risks (e.g., the well might be less productive when reopened) associated with this decision.
